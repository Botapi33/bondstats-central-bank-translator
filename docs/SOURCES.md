# Primary-source research basis

V1 mappings were designed from official central-bank implementation documentation, including:

- European Central Bank — Monetary policy operations / standing facilities.
- Federal Reserve Board — Interest on Reserve Balances, Standing Repo Facility, and implementation notes.
- Bank of Japan — Monetary Policy Measures, Complementary Deposit Facility, Complementary Lending Facility, and funds-supplying operations.

Before publishing additional institution-specific mappings, verify the current operational framework at the relevant central bank. A similar economic function is not enough to label two instruments equivalent.
